# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Raw Code to be a welcoming project for contributors of every background and skill level, including people making their first open-source contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and constructive in issues, reviews, and discussions.
- Assume good intent and explain feedback clearly.
- Keep disagreements technical and respectful.
- Help newcomers understand the codebase and contribution workflow.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language or exclusionary behavior
- Sharing someone else's private information without permission
- Posting secrets, exploit details, or unsafe instructions irresponsibly

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or block accounts that repeatedly violate this code of conduct.

If you are unsure how to raise a concern, open a short maintainer issue and ask for a private contact channel.
